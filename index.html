About

The heart of Sălaj County

Zalău is the capital and largest city of Sălaj County, set among the gentle hills of north-western Romania, between the Apuseni Mountains and the historic region of Maramureș. With a population of around 56,000, it blends small-city warmth with a rich layered past stretching back to Roman times.

Once known as Porolissum's neighbouring settlement and later a market town on important trade routes, today Zalău is a green, walkable city built around a central promenade, leafy parks, and a community proud of its Transylvanian heritage.

History

A journey through time

  1. 2nd c. AD

    Roman frontier

    Nearby Porolissum becomes one of the largest Roman military and civilian complexes in Dacia, guarding the empire's north-western frontier.

  2. 1473

    First written mention

    Zalău is documented as a settlement and grows into a notable market town with the right to hold fairs.

  3. 1571

    Free royal town

    Zalău receives privileges that strengthen its role as a regional centre of trade and crafts.

  4. 1918

    Union with Romania

    Following the Great Union, Zalău becomes part of modern Romania and the administrative seat of Sălaj.

  5. Today

    A modern county capital

    A growing city with industry, education, and culture — and a gateway to the natural beauty of Sălaj.

Attractions

What to see & do

🏛️

Porolissum

Explore the impressive ruins of this Roman castrum and town just outside the city — amphitheatre, gates, and temples on a panoramic hilltop.

🖼️

County Museum of History & Art

Discover artefacts from Porolissum and Sălaj's folk heritage in Zalău's well-regarded museum.

⚔️

Wesselényi Statue

The landmark monument honouring Baron Miklós Wesselényi stands proudly in the central square.

🌳

Central Park & Promenade

Stroll the green pedestrian heart of the city, lined with cafés, fountains, and shaded benches.

⛪

Churches of Zalău

Visit the Reformed Church and the Calvaria Church, reflecting the city's diverse spiritual history.

🥾

Sălaj's hills & nature

Use Zalău as a base for hiking, the Meseș Mountains, and the rural villages and wooden churches nearby.

Getting there

Zalău is reachable by road via the DN1F and is about 90 minutes from Cluj-Napoca, the nearest major airport.

Best time to go

Late spring to early autumn (May–September) offers warm weather ideal for exploring Porolissum and the surrounding hills.

Local flavour

Try Transylvanian and Sălaj specialities — hearty soups, smoked cheeses, and fresh pastries from the local market.
